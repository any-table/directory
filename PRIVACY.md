# Privacy

## What the directory stores

For each table: what the host enters in the listing form, the host's email address, and the dates it was published, edited, and last confirmed.

That's all. There are no accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking, and no advertising. The pages load nothing from other sites, except Cloudflare's spam check on the three forms that use it.

## What is public

Everything in a listing except the email address. Home tables show only their area. Public listings are also published every night, under CC0, in the directory's [GitHub repository](https://github.com/any-table/directory), so the list outlives this website. Email addresses are never published there or anywhere else.

## How the email address is used

Only to send the host the link to publish and manage the listing, reminders to confirm it, notice if it is hidden or removed, and messages from people who want to come. Custodians can't see it. It is never shared, sold, or used for anything else.

## Messages

When someone writes to a host, the message is forwarded to the host by email with the sender's address as the reply address. The host's address is shown to the sender only if the host replies. Messages aren't stored.

## Deleting your information

Removing a listing deletes it and its email address at once. Unpublished listings are deleted after two days, and hidden listings a year after they were last confirmed. Copies already published to GitHub stay in its history, which is why they never contain email addresses.

## Who runs it

The directory runs on Cloudflare, and email is sent through Resend. It is held in trust for the tables by the custodians named in the project's [governance record](https://anytable.org/governance/), under the rules in the [foundational document](https://anytable.org/foundational/).
